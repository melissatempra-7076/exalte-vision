A note to Joanna

Joanna,

I've spent some time researching the wellness market, emerging business models and, importantly, how AI is going to reshape the industry over the next decade.

As wellness information, content and personalisation become increasingly accessible through AI, value will shift towards trusted expertise, genuine community, distinctive experiences and the ability to reach, engage and retain customers. That's where I believe the real competitive advantage will sit.

And this is where things get interesting. exalte already has the foundations: a distinctive philosophy, an established network of practitioners and a growing wellness community. But I think one of its biggest competitive advantages could be sitting right in front of us. Could Reymond turn the dial? The hospitality and lifestyle relationships you've spent years building, particularly internationally, could provide an incredible platform for exalte to reach new audiences and scale.

Herein are two potential business models for exalte, both compelling in their own right, but potentially far more powerful together. They could build recurring revenue, expand distribution and create something that can scale well beyond your own time and resources.

I'd love to hear what resonates, what doesn't, and how this compares with the vision you have for exalte.

02 · The next decade
02

Where will value move in an AI-enabled world?

AI is rapidly changing how people find information, make decisions and choose services, and wellness is particularly exposed. People can already get personalised routines, nutrition guidance and meditation from digital tools in seconds. As those tools improve, general wellness content will become harder to differentiate or charge for on its own.

But technology doesn't remove the need for human connection, trusted judgement or meaningful real-world experiences. McKinsey's Future of Wellness research found people buying more in-person services, including boutique fitness and wellness retreats, and its 2026 preview points to growing demand for in-person experiences with others, combining community and connection with a more relaxing setting.

What AI is likely to make easier

  • Accessing general wellness information
  • Discovering and comparing services
  • Creating basic personalised routines
  • Bookings, communications and admin
  • Delivering digital content at scale

What remains hard to replicate

  • Trust in a carefully selected expert
  • Belonging and meaningful relationships
  • Exceptional experiences in beautiful places
  • Access to distinctive practitioners and communities
  • Personal attention and considered human judgement

This doesn't mean moving away from digital education. Content can become the way people discover exalte and come to trust it. But it raises a strategic question:

What if exalte's greatest long-term value isn't the information it provides, but the people, experiences and relationships it brings together?

Most of these bring digital content or a product into the hotel. exalte could offer something rarer: live, expert-led experiences, a curated practitioner network, and the relationships to put them in the right venues. Public examples don't disclose fees or revenue shares, so how such deals are structured is an important next step to explore.

What exalte experiences could look like

  • Movement and mindfulness in beautiful environments
  • Expert-led lunches, conversations and intimate gatherings
  • Destination retreats combining nourishment, movement and connection
  • Seasonal programs delivered through hotels and resorts
  • Experiences for corporate groups, private communities and premium brands

Why it suits exalte

exalte would own the formats, with partners providing venues, infrastructure and guests. Successful formats could run across properties, cities or countries, delivered by local practitioners to exalte's standards: an asset-light model that grows through partnerships rather than property or large teams.

Commercial models: paid experiences and retreats, corporate programs, co-branded partnerships, recurring hotel programming and, longer term, licensing of signature formats.

The scalable layer

exalte in-room rituals

Live experiences build prestige, but each one needs people in the room. In-room rituals could be developed once and offered across multiple rooms, properties and markets, creating a scalable revenue opportunity without requiring a practitioner to deliver every experience.

Demand is clear: a Hilton survey found 90% of travellers try to keep up their wellness routines on the road. Hotels already sell this, from Ritz-Carlton's sleep ritual with Longeva and Rosewood's sleep program across 20 hotels to COMO's in-room yoga and on-demand classes, and an in-room sleep ritual here at 1 Hotel Melbourne.

Four layers in every ritual

  1. A beautiful ritual kit. Co-branded with Australian makers, some of it to take home.
  2. exalte content. Short Pilates, breath and meditation sessions led by you and your experts, filmed in-suite and played via QR code or the room TV.
  3. Nourishment. Two or three 80/20 menu items created with the hotel's chef.
  4. An optional upgrade. An in-room session with an exalte practitioner, linking directly to the Collective.

Opportunity Two

exalte Collective

A trusted wellness ecosystem connecting people with exceptional practitioners, experiences, education and community.

People aren't short of wellness information. They're trying to work out whom to trust and where to start. Meanwhile, many excellent practitioners lack the brand, audience or relationships to grow. exalte could sit between the two.

Not an open directory, which is easy to copy, but a curated collective whose value lies in trust, standards, a loyal audience and premium venues. A first tangible expression could be small, guided monthly gatherings in partner venues.

05

A competitive advantage that lasts

Trends will change and technology will reshape the landscape. A business built to last ten years needs advantages that grow stronger as it grows.

Trusted experiences→Customers→Demand→Practitioners & partners→A more valuable exalte

  1. Trust and reputation

    Known for considered curation, credible practitioners and consistently excellent experiences. Trust takes years to build and is hard to copy.

  2. Relationships and distribution

    Exceptional hospitality and lifestyle partners, backed by long-term agreements. Your relationships are a meaningful head start.

  3. A distinctive practitioner collective

    Selected experts who embody the exalte philosophy, more valuable through shared standards and feedback.

  4. Signature experiences and IP

    Formats, programs and standards owned by exalte, repeatable by qualified partners without a large central team.

  5. Direct relationships and community

    An audience that chooses exalte, returns and recommends it. Perhaps the most valuable asset of all.

06

Growing step by step

With Reymond remaining an established business, exalte should grow in a way that fits how you want to spend your time, with each success creating something reusable.

Stage 1

Prove it

Find the experiences and practitioner relationships with the strongest demand, and test a few paid offerings.

Stage 2

Partner

Bring the best formats to selected hospitality, corporate and lifestyle partners as repeatable formats.

Stage 3

Deepen

Strengthen the collective, adding structured ways to access services, experiences and community.

Stage 4

Scale

Multi-property partnerships, licensing and membership, once demand and economics are proven.

Ideas worth testing in 2027

A signature experience

One paid experience combining movement, nourishment, connection and education. Measure satisfaction, profitability and return interest.

A hospitality pilot

One hotel where an exalte program or in-room ritual benefits both businesses. Test delivery, costs and how a deal could repeat.

A practitioner pilot

A small group of selected practitioners under a structured exalte proposition. Learn what they value and what customers will pay for.

A journey beyond the first visit

A welcome journey that invites course buyers, subscribers and guests back into the wider exalte world.

Built with AI from the start

Existing tools for communications, admin, insight and coordination, before investing in any custom platform.

The real purpose

Not only revenue, but learning which parts of exalte can become a repeatable, scalable business.

A final thought

You have something really special

A thoughtful philosophy, credible practitioners, experience creating events, strong brand-building skills and an established network across hospitality and lifestyle. Together, they open opportunities most new wellness businesses simply don't have.

With AI set to transform how people access information and services, there's a compelling case for building around what remains genuinely human: trust, connection, experience and belonging.

Perhaps the opportunity isn't simply to help people learn how to live well, but to create the people, places and experiences that help them actually do it.
